Play god over a tiny island colony. Make tools from sticks and mud, and make their lives better.

Genre
Creativity: sticks and mud
Platform
Virtual reality, Oculus Quest
Audience
College-age men interested in science

You watch over them from above

A small group of people is stranded on a deserted island. You take the role of their god, looking down from a bird's-eye view in VR.

Your goal is to create as many tools as you can that raise the colony's quality of life. Mud and trees give you your main materials, bricks and sticks.

Every tool grows the colony

  1. Two castaways and a palm tree.
  2. A family with a child, living in two huts with stick roofs.
  3. A bigger group celebrating under a tall tower.
